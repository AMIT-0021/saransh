# Saransh (सारांश) — Clinical Terms of Service & Institutional Terms and Conditions
**Operational Guidelines & Legal Liability Framework for Healthcare Facilities**
**Aligned with National Medical Commission (NMC) & CDSCO Medical Device Rules 2017**

---

## 1. Institutional Acceptance & Scope of Application
These Clinical Terms of Service and Institutional Terms & Conditions ("Terms") govern the institutional deployment and frontline utilization of **Project Saransh (सारांश)** ("the Platform") across authorized public and private healthcare facilities, including Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs), Sub-Centres / Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, and District Hospitals.

By accessing or operating the Platform, accredited healthcare workers (ASHA, ANM, Staff Nurses, and Medical Officers) and participating institutional entities agree to be bound by these Terms.

---

## 2. Regulatory Classification: Assistive Clinical Decision Support (SaMD)
1. **Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) Tier:**
   Under the **Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) Medical Device Rules 2017**, Saransh is classified as an **Assistive Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS / Class A - Low Risk)**.
2. **Non-Diagnostic Nature:**
   The Platform is strictly an **Intake Acceleration and Triage Prioritization Utility**. It **DOES NOT** formulate autonomous medical diagnoses, initiate pharmacological therapy, or replace formal clinical judgment.
3. **No Establishment of Direct AI-Patient Relationship:**
   Operating the Platform does not constitute the independent practice of medicine by an artificial intelligence entity.

---

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop Mandate & Medical Practitioner Accountability
In strict accordance with the **National Medical Commission (Registered Medical Practitioners - Professional Conduct) Regulations**:

1. **Absolute Clinical Primacy:**
   The licensed attending Medical Officer (Doctor) retains **100% legal, ethical, and clinical authority** over patient diagnosis, emergency intervention, admission, and referral.
2. **Mandatory Counter-Signature:**
   No triage report, FHIR R4 clinical summary, or National Health Mission (NHM) referral slip generated by Saransh is legally operative until an accredited Medical Officer with a valid **State Medical Council or NMC Registration Number** reviews, validates, and counter-signs the record.
3. **Right and Duty to Override:**
   Medical Officers are legally required to override any AI-generated priority recommendation if their independent bedside examination reveals discordant clinical signs.

---

## 4. Emergency Care & Resuscitation Exemption
* **Immediate Life-Threat Protocol:**
  In scenarios involving profound circulatory shock, respiratory arrest, major exsanguinating hemorrhage, or active convulsions, frontline workers **MUST NOT DELAY** patient transfer to the emergency bay to perform digital intake.
* **Deterministic Red Line Guarantee:**
  When vital telemetry is entered, non-AI deterministic physiological guardrails ($\text{SpO}_2 < 90\%$, Systolic BP $> 180\text{ mmHg}$) automatically trigger an emergency chime and lock the patient into the 🔴 RED lane. However, digital logging is strictly secondary to immediate physical resuscitation.

---

## 5. Frontline Operator Protocols (ASHA & ANM Personnel)
1. **Permissible Usage:**
   Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHA) and Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANM) are authorized to:
   - Verify patient 14-digit ABHA IDs.
   - Record vernacular patient voice complaints in local languages (Odia, Hindi).
   - Log vital signs via digital non-invasive equipment.
   - Assist the patient in pinpointing symptoms on the 2D anatomical map.
2. **Impermissible Usage:**
   Frontline operators are strictly prohibited from modifying triage categories, discharging patients, or altering clinical notes without Medical Officer authorization.

---

## 6. Data Ownership, Consent & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance
1. **Patient Data Ownership:**
   All health data remains the statutory property of the patient, administered under the custody of the respective State Department of Health & Family Welfare.
2. **Consent Requirement:**
   Data capture requires affirmative verbal or written patient consent, except where life-saving interventions apply under the emergency exemption of **Section 7(a) of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023**.
3. **Edge Processing & Anonymization:**
   Institutional endpoints must enforce client-side PII stripping prior to any remote cloud synthesis.

---

## 7. Limitation of Technical Liability & Offline Operation
1. **Connectivity Disruptions:**
   The Platform provides offline caching via browser IndexedDB and local SQLite. Neither the development team nor institutional hosts shall be held liable for delayed data synchronizations caused by regional power outages or telecommunications blackouts.
2. **Third-Party Integrations:**
   While Saransh integrates with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and National Health Systems Resource Centre (NHSRC) standards, uptime of third-party NHA gateways is governed by respective government SLAs.

---

These Terms shall be construed and governed in accordance with the laws of the **Republic of India**, including the **Information Technology Act 2000**, **DPDP Act 2023**, and the regulations of the **National Medical Commission (NMC)**. Any disputes arising out of the software implementation shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Odisha, India.
