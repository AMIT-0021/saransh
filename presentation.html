The Healthcare Challenge

Frontline OPD Breakdown in Public Facilities

Why traditional intake methods fail frontline healthcare workers and patients every day

🚨 Overcrowded & Chaotic Queues

Patients queue on a first-come, first-served basis. Critical myocardial infarctions and acute pediatric respiratory distress wait behind simple coughs and prescription refills.

  • No early triage sorting before meeting the doctor
  • "Golden Hour" lost in registration queues
  • Frontline ASHA/ANM workers overwhelmed by paperwork
🗣️ Dialect & Linguistic Disconnect

Rural patients describe symptoms using colloquial metaphors and regional idioms rather than standard medical terminology.

  • "Chhati re bhara laagu chi" (Odia: Chest heaviness / Ischemia)
  • "Matha ghurauchi, chhati dhada dhada" (Palpitations / Tachycardia)
  • Standard Western NLP tools misclassify or fail completely
📉 Fractured Referral Continuum

When patients are escalated from rural PHCs to District or Tertiary Hospitals (e.g. AIIMS or SCB Cuttack), records are lost.

  • Handwritten, illegible prescription chits
  • Zero digital continuity or ABHA integration
  • Doctors at receiving tertiary centres repeat vitals from scratch

Safety-First Rubric (20% Evaluation Weight)

The Deterministic Red-Line Engine

AI assists, but hard physiological boundaries guarantee patient safety with zero hallucination risk

🫀 Respiratory & Hemodynamic

Instant RED classification if vitals breach critical life-threatening thresholds:

IF SpO2 < 90% → LANE: RED (Hypoxia Alert) IF Systolic BP > 180 mmHg → LANE: RED (Hypertensive Crisis) IF Heart Rate > 130 or < 40 bpm → LANE: RED (Cardiac Arrhythmia)
⚡ Symptom Red-Flag Overrides

Pre-programmed clinical keywords force high priority regardless of user input:

  • Crushing central chest pain radiating to left jaw
  • Acute unilateral facial droop or hemiplegia (FAST stroke)
  • Stridor / severe pediatric respiratory retractions
  • Post-trauma hemorrhage with altered sensorium
🛡️ Audit Trails & Explainability

Every triage decision has full transparent reasoning displayed to the medical officer:

Reasoning Generated: "Deterministic Rule #3 Triggered: Patient SpO2 is 88% on room air. Immediate High-Flow Oxygen & Emergency ECG required."

Technical Architecture

Modern, Resilient & Offline-First Stack

Engineered for zero downtime in rural clinics with spotty broadband connectivity

Frontend & Intake Layer
  • React 18 + Vite: Ultra-fast 60 FPS responsive UI optimized for low-end tablets and ASHA kiosks.
  • Lucide Medical Icons & Tailwind CSS: High contrast dark/light theme readable in bright sunlight.
  • IndexedDB Offline Queue: Patient intake proceeds without internet; syncs automatically on reconnect.
  • Interactive 2D Canvas: SVG anatomical mapping with front/back toggle and pain severity sliders.
Backend & Inference Engine
  • FastAPI (Python 3.14): Asynchronous, typed endpoints with Pydantic v2 schemas and OpenAPI docs.
  • Dual-Mode AI Engine: Cloud Gemini 2.5 Flash for nuanced vernacular speech + local rule engine fallback.
  • Deterministic Gatekeeper: Rules execute before and after LLM inference to validate physiological bounds.
  • SQLite / PostgreSQL: Relational data models with automatic FHIR R4 Bundle conversion.
